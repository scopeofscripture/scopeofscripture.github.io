Ancient Man Knew More Than We Do!

Across the ancient world, physical artifacts, written records, and oral traditions converge on a striking possibility: that early civilizations possessed scientific and technological knowledge in some respects superior to our own — knowledge later lost, suppressed, or simply forgotten.

Nobel laureate physicist Frederick Soddy once posed the question directly: could ancient civilizations have attained "not only our present knowledge, but a power hitherto unmastered by us"?

The companion eBook catalogues 64 specific aspects of ancient science and technology that continue to challenge and, in places, exceed modern understanding.
