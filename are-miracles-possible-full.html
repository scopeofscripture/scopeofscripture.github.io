Are Miracles Possible?

SUPERSTITION OR FACT

Some doubt some believe I have experienced both. As a young adult I was an atheist who believed that science had proved that God did not exist and for me at that time miracles where certainly fiction. But by the time I reached 29 my life changed and I experienced my first miracle. I would call it the greatest miracle that anyone can ever experience and praise the Lord and bless His holy name everyone can experience this miracle. It is the miracle of the new birth described by Jesus in John 3. And it offers hope in this life and the one to come. Following this miracle I have experienced many , many more as a disciple of Christ.

At times we hear of astonishing things that occur to people. Sometimes such accounts almost strain credibility. However, events that we might call miracles are constantly occurring. Whether one accepts them as such depends upon one's attitude concerning the existence of God. If one accepts the reality of a Almighty being who created us and directs the universe, it may make sense. What we might call "supernatural" could be perfectly natural to a Supreme Being, quite natural to His power. If He created laws of nature, might He not override such laws? Using even natural phenomena in a "supernatural" way? Could I suggest that even when supernatural events find explanation in the framework of understood natural phenomena, does that lessen the divine, miraculous wisdom behind them? May I also suggest that much that we attribute to chance is due the all-wise and loving providence of our Creator, who works circumstances together for the best good of those who commit their lives to follow His ways and place their trust in Him.

And in addition to this and further to the point for those who have been reconciled to this Almighty God through His generous and precious son the Lord Jesus Christ, There is provision for can in all honesty be called the miraculous. I have experienced many such examples in may own life.

If love to hear the testimony of reports that inspire and encourage and that fill you with confidence in a heavenly Father who loves you very much, then the e Book was that a miracle should hit the spot. It contains over 60 documented miracles.
