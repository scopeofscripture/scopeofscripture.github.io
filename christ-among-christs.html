Is Jesus Merely a Christ Among Many Christs?

Jesus is merely a Christ among Many Christs

Jesus' name encoded in the Old Testament

In 1994, scholars at the Hebrew University and Jerusalem College of Technology announced they had discovered a hidden code in the Bible that appeared to reveal the details of events that took place thousands of years after the Bible was written amazingly encoded below the surface but within the body of text.

The code was discovered by Dr Eliyahu Rips, a world expert mathematician in quantum physics. It was later confirmed by famous mathematicians at Yale, Harvard and Hebrew University's and verified by a senior code-breaker at the United States Department of Defence. Commonly referred to as a "skipping" code, this sub-surface code was discovered in the original language version of the Old Testament, that is, the Hebrew.

Hidden under the plain surface text, was found information in the form of equidistant letter sequences. Meaning, letters spaced at regular, equal distances throughout the text, spelled words containing another message.

The code is so intricate, complex, and clever that it is certainly well beyond human intelligence to create. The amount of information encoded into a limited text has been proven to be outside the capacity of any individual or group to create, whatever computing resources are available. We are dealing with highly sophisticated word arrangements, that are invisible without a computer. It's the equivalent of a book constructed with several different layers of coded information hidden under the text, each layer independent of the other and each complete in itself.
