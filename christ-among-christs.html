Jesus Is Merely a Christ Among Many Christs?

In 1994, mathematicians at Hebrew University and Jerusalem College of Technology confirmed a startling discovery: equidistant letter sequences encoded beneath the plain Hebrew text of the Old Testament, spelling out names and events centuries before they occurred.

In Isaiah 53 — a passage prophesying the Messiah — the phrase "Jesus is my name" appears encoded at 20-letter intervals. Elsewhere, dozens of names connected to Jesus's crucifixion (Nazareth, Pilate, Caiaphas, the disciples) are found encoded within the same prophetic passage, written some 700 years before those people were born.

Despite parallels drawn between Jesus and figures like Krishna, Mithra, and Osiris, the weight of textual, prophetic, and archaeological evidence points toward a stolen identity — ancient pagan "christs" borrowing from a single original prophecy, not the reverse.
