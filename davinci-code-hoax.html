Archaeological Discoveries

A Judgement From God — Summary of a Damaged Planet

When the Ark ran aground on the mountains of Turkey, its occupants faced a world transformed beyond recognition — a desolate, rock-strewn landscape in place of the once-lush pre-Flood earth, with entirely new and harsher climate zones.

Ararat became the cradle of the new post-Flood civilization, the starting point for two great dispersions: an initial rapid migration while humanity still shared one language, and a later, forced scattering following the confusion of tongues at Babel.

Ten independent geological and historical "clocks" — from glacier growth to river delta formation to written human records — all point to a major planetary event roughly 4,000 years ago, consistent with the biblical Flood and the age-adjustment processes still unfolding today.
