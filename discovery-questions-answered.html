Archaeological Discoveries

Noah's Ark Found! Five Major Discovery Claims

Since the late 1970s, a series of major archaeological claims have been made and disputed: the discovery of Noah's Ark in Turkey, the ruins of Sodom and Gomorrah in Israel, the true site of the Red Sea crossing, the real Mount Sinai, and the Ark of the Covenant itself.

Each claim has faced sustained skepticism — even within Christian circles — despite what researchers describe as thorough testing and confirmation at each site. Objections have ranged from questions of authenticity to outright dismissal without site visits.

The companion volume addresses every major objection and criticism raised against each of these five discoveries directly, point by point, allowing readers to weigh the evidence for themselves.
