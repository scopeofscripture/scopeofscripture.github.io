Endless Punishment — Heathen Origin or Biblical Reality?

Belief in a place of endless torment was widespread among the ancient pagans — the Greeks, Romans and Egyptians all described an underworld ruled by fire, monstrous guardians, and unending torment, in vivid detail preserved in works like Virgil's Aeneid.

Strikingly, many popular Christian depictions of hell closely echo these pagan descriptions rather than a careful reading of Scripture. This raises an important question: where did the dogma of eternal conscious torment actually originate, and does the Bible itself teach it?

For a concise, unbiased biblical summary of what man is, what hell actually is, and a true examination of the account of the Rich Man and Lazarus, the companion eBook explores this often-misunderstood doctrine in full.
