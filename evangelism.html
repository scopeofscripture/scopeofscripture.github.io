The Urgent Call to Evangelism

In a world brimming with noise, distraction, and competing narratives, the church faces a question that has echoed across centuries: why does evangelism matter? The answer is not merely pragmatic — it is deeply theological, rooted in the very heart of God and the mission He entrusted to His people.

Evangelism is not an optional activity for a select few "gifted" believers. It is the heartbeat of the Christian faith, the natural overflow of a life transformed by grace. When we truly grasp what Christ has done for us, sharing that good news becomes not a burden, but a joy.
