The Extermination Plot

Evolutionary theory's influence has extended well beyond the natural sciences, shaping 20th-century political ideologies that treated entire populations as expendable in pursuit of a perceived societal advancement.

Historical documentation, including U.S. national security policy papers from the 1970s, reveals population-reduction strategies pursued at the highest levels of government, tied to control of global resources and economic systems.

The companion eBook traces this thread through history in far greater depth, connecting ideological, political, and religious developments across two centuries.
