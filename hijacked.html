Hijacked — Will You Be Rescued?

Most people are unaware that our world has been taken captive — its courts, governments, science, education, media, and even mainstream religion infiltrated by forces hostile to mankind. Like a kidnap victim who comes to identify with a captor, humanity has grown so used to this condition that it no longer recognizes it for what it is.

This is not a matter of religion versus non-religion. Scripture describes it plainly: "We wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world." (Ephesians 6:12) Ancient civilizations across the globe — from Sumeria to China to the Americas — share strikingly similar accounts of a primeval "Fall," a rebellion in the unseen realm that left mankind alienated from the Creator.

Babylonian, Egyptian and Mexican traditions alike describe a longing for rescue from this condition. Despite the schemes at work behind world events, the Almighty has provided — and continues to provide — a way of escape into a restored world free of sin and death, a promise first given at the dawn of human history and unfolding toward its conclusion.
