Is Islam Mentioned in the Bible?

When I first announced that some old prophecies possibly foretold what Islam will do, there were a few skeptics. But before I go any further let's consider what a man in the mid-19th century suggested based on his understanding of scripture. At the time, the Muslim Ottoman empire was still a leading world power. And European nations were afraid. Islam had been striking fear into the heart of Europe for 400 years.

Then in 1838 a scholar named Josiah Litch, announced publicly that the mighty Ottoman Empire "will lose supremacy on August 11, 1840." Many thought what an OUTRAGEOUS comment and an absurd speculation on PROPHECY. Would you stick your neck out like this man did? He wasn't suggesting this to a few friends the whole world knew he had predicted it and most people found it very hard to accept what Litch was saying.

Not only did Litch announce that this unlikely event would take place "two years from now, in 1840," but more than that, he pushed his prophecy further: "It is going to happen in the month August that year. And not just in August, but on the 11th day of August... in that very year, month, and day, and at no other time!"

Can you imagine the loud laughter of the skeptics? The cries went up, "Oh, come off it! Don't be ridiculous! That Muslim empire has been around for hundreds of years. They're so powerful, striking terror into Europe. And you're saying we won't have to deal with them in two years? Get real!" As the time approached, thousands watched with intense interest. Non-believers waited to see the prophecy fail. August 11 dawned. Can you imagine the conversation that Josiah Litch and his family were having that morning at breakfast. "Today is the day," says Josiah. "Today the Ottoman empire will lose its supremacy." Or perhaps his heart experienced a moment of doubt, did I really hear from God, are the scriptures really making this claim.

Steadying himself and reassuring himself perhaps he thought, I am absolutely certain it is going to happen, I have been over it so many times and checked and re-checked the prophecy so many times. It is clear and certain.

As the day progressed, on the other side of the world, delegates of five nations come together for an historic meeting. The four great powers of Europe, - Britain, Russia, Prussia and Austria - met with the Sultan of the Turkish Empire.

A war had broken out between Mehemet Ali, Pasha of Egypt and the Sultan's subjects in Syria. This threatened the integrity of the Ottoman Empire. Feeling vulnerable, not sure he could successfully handle the situation, the Sultan sensed his throne was in danger. There was a fear also that the security of Europe might come under threat.

So the Sultan of Turkey asked the four European powers - Britain, Russia, Prussia and Austria - to intervene. And so it was that on August 11, 1840, the Turkish leader voluntarily signs a paper that takes away the independence and power of the Turkish Sultan forever. Turkish supremacy is passed to these four European powers, who have come together in a "coalition of the willing".

So now the Muslim Ottoman empire, which for almost four centuries had tormented the nations of Europe, on this very day loses its supremacy. On the very year, month and day that Josiah Litch has predicted, the newspapers announce:

"The Ottoman empire loses its power"
