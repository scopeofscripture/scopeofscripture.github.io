Is Islam Mentioned in the Bible?

In 1838, scholar Josiah Litch made an extraordinary public prediction based on Bible prophecy: that the powerful Ottoman Empire would lose its supremacy on a specific date — August 11, 1840. Skeptics scoffed. Two years later, on that exact day, the Sultan of Turkey signed away his empire's independence to four European powers.

This fulfilled timing, drawn from ancient prophecy, is understood by some scholars to represent only the second part of a larger three-part prophecy — one that some believe has continued unfolding in recent decades through geopolitical events in Europe, Turkey, and the Middle East.

The full study traces this prophetic thread from its 19th-century fulfillment through to unfolding modern events, including the stated ambitions of the Ottoman Caliphate's would-be successors.
