Archaeological Discoveries

Evolution Theory Confronted — Sudden Global Civilization

Egypt, Sumeria, the Indus Valley civilization, the Maya, and the megalith builders of northwest Europe all appear in the archaeological record suddenly and fully developed — with no trace of gradual evolution from primitive beginnings.

Tracing the roots of language, writing systems, agriculture, and anthropology consistently points back to a single origin in the Middle East highlands — precisely where biblical tradition places the landing of Noah's Ark, in the mountains of Ararat.

The most reasonable explanation: these post-Flood civilizations inherited advanced knowledge from the world destroyed in the Deluge, rather than evolving it independently — a conclusion that directly confronts the standard evolutionary timeline.
