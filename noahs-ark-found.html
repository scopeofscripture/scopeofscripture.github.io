Evidence to Shock a Post-Christian Culture to Its Core

A HEATED BATTLE RAGES OVER 5 MAJOR DISCOVERY CLAIMS

In the late 1970s and continuing into the 1980s and beyond a series of significant discoveries were made by amateur archaeologist Ron Wyatt. These claims were soon disputed and challenged but it wasn't long before these said discoveries were professionally proven true rather than false. What is fascinating about these discoveries is that they confirm events in Earth's history that to a large extend today are regarded as myth or legend.

These finds come at a time when mankind in the countries that have had a history with the God of the Bible, are mostly apostate. Secular humanism has run rampant and it's not just the secularist today that consigns the bible to myth but also many who claim to still be "Christian" do likewise. Concerning these major events in Earth history where God has intervened in a miraculous way it is explained away in a legendary manner. Sad but true and its not just the un assuming lukewarm church goer or the adamant secular humanist scientist, but some in the Creationist Christian camp reject these monumental findings as well.
