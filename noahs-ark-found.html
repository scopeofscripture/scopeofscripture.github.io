Noah's Ark Found! Resistivity Scans Reveal a Ship

High-altitude photography first revealed a boat-shaped object high in the mountains of Ararat in the late 1950s. Decades of investigation followed, including groundbreaking work by researcher Ron Wyatt beginning in 1977, using ground-penetrating radar and core drilling to reveal a massive man-made structure over 6,000 feet up.

In 2014, deep-penetration Electrical Resistivity Tomography scans mapped the structure in full 3D. The results revealed three distinct internal deck floors — matching Genesis 6:16's instruction that the Ark have "lower, second, and third stories" — along with a central stairway connecting them, exactly as would be expected in a vessel of this design.

Measured in the ancient cubit (20.6 inches), the structure's internal length, width, and height match the biblical dimensions of Noah's Ark — 300 by 50 by 30 cubits — precisely, with no fractional remainders, strong evidence of deliberate, ancient construction.
