Project India

Widows and Orphans of Kerala

It is our goal to work with existing widows and orphans in the region of Kerala, India. This project is in its infancy, and we welcome all the help we can get. We intend to keep an up-to-date progress report to advise of developments. Your prayers and financial support for this project are very much appreciated.
