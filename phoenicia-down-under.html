Etymology

In the Beginning — One God and One Tongue

Petroglyphs discovered in Colorado were found to be virtually identical to ancient inscriptions independently documented in Israel's Negev desert, South Australia, Yemen, Chile, and the British Isles — suggesting a single original global language, since termed "The First Tongue."

Traditions from Sumeria to Mesoamerica independently recall a time when "the whole universe spoke in one tongue," followed by a sudden, dramatic confusion of languages and a scattering of peoples from a central point — matching the biblical account of Babel.

Translations of these earliest inscriptions reveal not polytheism but a original monotheistic faith, centered on a single Creator — a pattern of religious degeneration into multiple gods over time, rather than the reverse evolutionary progression commonly assumed.
