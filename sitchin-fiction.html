Radiometric Dating — Can It Be Trusted?

Dating methods are often presented as unquestionable science, yet the record tells a different story. Human remains near New Orleans were dated at 57,000 years old — until wood from a 19th-century flatboat found at a deeper layer collapsed that figure to under 200 years.

Rock samples from an 1801 Hawaiian lava flow, known to be under 200 years old, returned radiometric ages ranging from 160 million to 3 billion years depending on the method used. Similar contradictions appear worldwide, with the same rock samples yielding wildly different ages by different dating techniques.

Carbon dating's own developer, Willard Libby, later admitted the method becomes unreliable beyond roughly 4,000–5,000 years. Beyond that point — coincidentally right around the biblical date of the Flood — there is no independent historical record against which to check it.
