Etymology

The Truth Behind Star Signs

Isolated ancient cultures worldwide — Romans, Hebrews, Greeks, and Indians — independently gave the same constellation the same meaning: "Virgin." This consistency across unconnected civilizations points to a single common origin for the zodiac, long before astrology as we know it existed.

Careful research has catalogued no fewer than 116 parallels between the pictures and names found on ancient star charts and the messianic prophecies of the Hebrew Scriptures — both telling, in different form, the same story of a coming Deliverer who would defeat a serpent-adversary and restore peace to the earth.

Calculations based on the position of the stars when the earliest zodiac was drawn point to roughly 4000 BC — thousands of years before the various pagan "christ" legends of India, Mexico, Greece, and Rome, suggesting the prophecy of a Messiah was known to humanity from a very early date.
