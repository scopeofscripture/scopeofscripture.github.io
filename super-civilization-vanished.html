Archaeological Discoveries

A Super Civilization That Vanished So Completely

History's destructive forces have erased far more than commonly realized. The Great Library of Alexandria, once holding a million volumes, was destroyed in stages — Julius Caesar alone burned 700,000 scrolls in a single act. Similar losses befell libraries in Carthage, Pergamos, and Constantinople.

Vast, unexplored regions remain even today — the Amazon basin alone contains 50,000 miles of navigable rivers and an estimated 16,000 tributaries, with ancient ruins reported and then swallowed again by jungle within a single year.

The evidence suggests a highly advanced global culture existed some 5,000 years ago, reaching from Siberia to Antarctica — one whose near-total disappearance is itself consistent with a catastrophic, world-altering event like the Flood.
