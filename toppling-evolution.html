Toppling Evolution One Fallacy at a Time

Despite over a century of searching since Darwin, the fossil record has consistently failed to produce clear transitional forms between species. Leading paleontologists — including those firmly committed to evolutionary theory — have openly admitted this gap.

Dr. Colin Patterson of the British Museum stated plainly that he could not make "a watertight argument" for even a single transitional fossil. The proposed solution, "punctuated equilibrium," suggests evolutionary leaps happened too fast to leave fossil evidence — an explanation with no supporting mechanism or observed example.

Even rapid mutation, as observed in viruses evolving a million times faster than typical DNA, has never produced anything beyond variations within the same organism — never a new one.
