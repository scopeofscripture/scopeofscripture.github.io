A Note on Our Video Content

Please note the various videos are for the purpose of highlighting biblical facts and cultural conditions, and are not intended to convey a certain theological perspective or promote a particular denomination.

Therefore it is recommended to view the content with an open mind, in accordance with the biblical admonition in Acts 17:11:

"...they received the word with all readiness of mind, and searched the scriptures daily, whether those things were so."

And 1 Thessalonians 5:21:

"Prove all things; hold fast that which is good."
